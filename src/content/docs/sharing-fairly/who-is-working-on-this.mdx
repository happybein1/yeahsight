Many groups already work on pieces of the path described in [Making the Transition](/sharing-fairly/making-the-transition/) and [The Transition in Practice](/sharing-fairly/transition-in-practice/). Each focuses on one piece, in one or several countries. This directory brings them together, so you can find the ones near you and see how their work fits the bigger picture.

Use the search box and filters to narrow the list. International and European organisations come first, then each country, with networks at the top. Networks connect national groups and keep their own member lists, so they are a good place to start if your country isn't listed yet.

**What gets listed:** organisations that are active, independent of political parties, and working on at least one piece of the plan (tax justice, inheritance and wealth, basic income, citizens' assemblies, carbon pricing). Being listed doesn't mean they endorse YeahSight, or that YeahSight agrees with all their positions.

**Missing an organisation?** Use the **Contact us** button at the bottom of the page. The list starts with international and European networks and with France, Belgium, the Netherlands and Germany, and grows over time.
