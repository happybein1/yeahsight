## Why it matters

The way society meets needs is changing fast. A few long-term forces will change work, resources and how people live together, whatever anyone decides.

## Articles, in order

1. [Climate Change](/forces/climate-change/): what is warming the planet, how far it has gone, and what it changes for people.
2. [The Energy Transition](/forces/clean-safe-energy/): from fossil fuels to clean energy, and what it changes.
3. [Managing Regional Migration](/forces/managing-regional-migration/): why people move, and how societies can manage it fairly.
4. [Population Change and Ageing](/forces/population-change/): fewer children, longer lives, and what that means for care, pensions and work.
5. [Enhanced Automation](/forces/enhanced-automation/): what machines are taking over, and how work will change.
6. [Impact of Artificial Intelligence](/forces/impact-of-artificial-intelligence/): what AI can do now, and its effects on economy and society.
7. [Life After Automation](/forces/life-after-automation/): when machines do most productive work, the challenge shifts to purpose and control.
8. [Big Risks: Pandemics, Nuclear War, Advanced AI](/forces/big-risks/): rare events that could set humanity back for generations, and what lowers the risk.

## How they connect

Climate change reaches into all the others, and energy is the main answer to it. Migration and population change alter the people a society works with; automation and AI change how the work gets done; Life After Automation asks what that means for people's needs, especially meaning and autonomy.

Big Risks closes the section: unlikely in any given year, but they could undo much of the rest.
