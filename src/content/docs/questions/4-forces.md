## Why it matters

The way society meets needs is changing fast. A few long-term forces will change work, resources and how people live together, whatever anyone decides.

## Articles, in order

1. [The Energy Transition](/forces/clean-safe-energy/): from fossil fuels to clean energy, and what it changes.
2. [Managing Regional Migration](/forces/managing-regional-migration/): why people move, and how societies can manage it fairly.
3. [Enhanced Automation](/forces/enhanced-automation/): what machines are taking over, and how work will change.
4. [Impact of Artificial Intelligence](/forces/impact-of-artificial-intelligence/): what AI can do now, and its effects on economy and society.
5. [Life After Automation](/forces/life-after-automation/): when machines do most productive work, the challenge shifts to purpose and control.

## How they connect

Energy and migration change the resources and the people a society works with; automation and AI change how the work gets done; Life After Automation asks what that means for people's needs, especially meaning and autonomy.
