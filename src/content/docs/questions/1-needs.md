## Why this question comes first

Every judgement about society needs a measure. YeahSight uses people's needs: a society works well when the people in it can meet them.

## The needs

Five needs are shared by every person, described on YeahCore: physical health and energy, safety and security, social connection, autonomy, and meaning and purpose.

- [Human needs](https://yeahcore.happybein.com/decision-foundation/find-your-directional-purpose/humans-physiological-needs/) (on YeahCore): what each need is, and how they relate.

## How this connects

These needs are the yardstick for every following question: whether resources are enough (2), whether society delivers them (3), what the big forces put at risk (4), and which solutions (5) and causes (6) help most.
