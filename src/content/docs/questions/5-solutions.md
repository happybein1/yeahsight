## Why it matters

Questions 1 to 4 set out the challenges: meeting everyone's needs, with limited resources, while big forces reshape society. These articles are HappyBein's recommendations, each with its goal, evidence, trade-offs and objections.

## Articles, in order

**Living within limits**

1. [Plant-Based Consumption](/forces/plant-based-consumption/): the environmental, ethical and health case for eating more plants.
2. [Fair Space Sharing](/sharing-fairly/fair-space-sharing/): how land could be shared between people and nature.

**Sharing fairly**

3. [Universal Allowance](/sharing-fairly/universal-allowance/): a guaranteed budget for basic needs: food, housing, health, energy.
4. [Inheritance](/sharing-fairly/inheritance/): a lifetime cap on what each person can inherit, and a fair start for everyone.
5. [Making the Transition](/sharing-fairly/making-the-transition/): how to get there step by step, without leaving people behind.
6. [The Transition in Practice](/sharing-fairly/transition-in-practice/): what if one country goes first, who runs the common fund, the main objections.

## How they connect

Eating more plants frees land, which can go back to nature (1 → 2). The inheritance cap funds a starting capital, and together with the allowance it guarantees everyone's basic needs (3 + 4). The two transition articles show the order and pace for all of it (5 → 6).
