## Why it matters

Not every solution can be pursued at once, and not every cause gives the same result for the same effort. This question brings the solutions together into priorities, and shows who is already working on each.

## Articles, in order

1. [Vision & Roadmap](/vision-roadmap/): the recommendations combined into six concrete steps.
2. [Who's Working on This](/sharing-fairly/who-is-working-on-this/): organisations already active on each step, to join or support.

## How they connect

The roadmap says what to do and in what order; the directory says who is doing it, so a reader can choose where to put their time, money or voice.
