<p class="yv-lead">Most of today's financial unfairness was inherited, not chosen. Wealth keeps earning more wealth, and passes from one generation to the next.</p>

<h2 class="yv-half">🙋 What I can do</h2>

<div class="yv-path">
<div class="yv-pstep"><div class="yv-pt"><span class="yv-n">1</span>Understand</div><p>Read <strong>Social Redistribution</strong>, or listen to it.</p><a class="yv-btn" href="/sharing-fairly/social-redistribution/">Read · 5 min</a></div>
<div class="yv-pstep"><div class="yv-pt"><span class="yv-n">2</span>Try today</div><p>Find out how taxes and public services work where you live, and what they pay for. It makes the debate much easier to follow.</p></div>
<div class="yv-pstep"><div class="yv-pt"><span class="yv-n">3</span>Keep it going</div><p>Support groups working on fair taxes, such as the <a href="https://taxjustice.net/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Tax Justice Network</a>, or on a basic income, such as <a href="https://basicincome.org/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">BIEN</a>.</p></div>
</div>

<h2 class="yv-half">🤝 What we can do as a society</h2>

<div class="yv-path">
<div class="yv-pstep"><div class="yv-pt"><span class="yv-n">1</span>The idea</div><p><strong>A secure floor, and a fair start for everyone.</strong> HappyBein proposes a slow, announced path: a basic-needs allowance first, then fairer taxes on new wealth like large inheritances, then a common fund that pays for everyone's start in life.</p><a class="yv-btn yv-btn--ghost" href="/sharing-fairly/making-the-transition/">From Inherited Wealth to a Fair Start</a></div>
<div class="yv-pstep"><div class="yv-pt"><span class="yv-n">2</span>What it would change</div><p>Across rich countries, taxes and transfers already reduce income inequality by about a quarter. Ordinary savings, pensions and family homes stay protected all along the way.</p></div>
<div class="yv-pstep"><div class="yv-pt"><span class="yv-n">3</span>How to push for it</div><p>Vote for fair taxes on very large fortunes and inheritances, and support the organisations listed in Who's Working on This.</p></div>
</div>
