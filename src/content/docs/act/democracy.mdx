<p class="yv-lead">Democracy only works if people can get reliable information and broadly trust each other. Both are under pressure.</p>

<h2 class="yv-half">🙋 What I can do</h2>

<div class="yv-path">
<div class="yv-pstep"><div class="yv-pt"><span class="yv-n">1</span>Understand</div><p>Read <strong>Democracy and Reliable Information</strong>, or listen to it.</p><a class="yv-btn" href="/how-society-works/democracy-and-information/">Read · 3 min</a></div>
<div class="yv-pstep"><div class="yv-pt"><span class="yv-n">2</span>Try today</div><p>Before you share something that makes you angry, take ten seconds to check where it comes from. And vote in every election, local ones too.</p></div>
<div class="yv-pstep"><div class="yv-pt"><span class="yv-n">3</span>Keep it going</div><p>Follow at least one reliable news source you pay for or support, and talk with people who think differently, calmly.</p></div>
</div>

<h2 class="yv-half">🤝 What we can do as a society</h2>

<div class="yv-path">
<div class="yv-pstep"><div class="yv-pt"><span class="yv-n">1</span>The idea</div><p><strong>Citizens' assemblies, and news people can trust.</strong> Groups of citizens drawn by lot, given time and good information, to advise on hard questions. And strong, independent public media.</p><a class="yv-btn yv-btn--ghost" href="/how-society-works/how-societies-change/">How Societies Change</a></div>
<div class="yv-pstep"><div class="yv-pt"><span class="yv-n">2</span>What it would change</div><p>Citizens' assemblies have helped countries such as Ireland move forward on questions politicians had avoided for years.</p></div>
<div class="yv-pstep"><div class="yv-pt"><span class="yv-n">3</span>How to push for it</div><p>Ask your city or country for a citizens' assembly, and support groups working on it, such as the <a href="https://www.sortitionfoundation.org/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Sortition Foundation</a> or <a href="https://democracyrd.org/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Democracy R&D</a>.</p></div>
</div>
