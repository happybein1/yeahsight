<p class="yv-lead">Livestock uses about three quarters of farmland for under a fifth of our calories, and tens of billions of land animals are farmed each year. Eating more plants is one of the biggest changes anyone can make.</p>

<h2 class="yv-half">🙋 What I can do</h2>

<div class="yv-path">
<div class="yv-pstep"><div class="yv-pt"><span class="yv-n">1</span>Understand</div><p>Read <strong>Plant-Based Consumption</strong>, or listen to it.</p><a class="yv-btn" href="/forces/plant-based-consumption/">Read · 3 min</a></div>
<div class="yv-pstep"><div class="yv-pt"><span class="yv-n">2</span>Try today</div><p>Replace one meat meal with a plant-based one, especially beef. For animals, chicken and eggs matter most, because they involve far more animals per meal.</p></div>
<div class="yv-pstep"><div class="yv-pt"><span class="yv-n">3</span>Keep it going</div><p>Make it a habit, a few meals a week at first. Small donations to campaigns for farmed animals, such as <a href="https://thehumaneleague.org/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">The Humane League</a>, bring measurable results.</p></div>
</div>

<h2 class="yv-half">🤝 What we can do as a society</h2>

<div class="yv-path">
<div class="yv-pstep"><div class="yv-pt"><span class="yv-n">1</span>The idea</div><p><strong>Less farmland, more space for nature.</strong> With fewer animals farmed, much less land is needed for food. HappyBein proposes that, over time, people use a fixed share of the land, and nature gets the rest back.</p><a class="yv-btn yv-btn--ghost" href="/sharing-fairly/fair-space-sharing/">Fair Space Sharing</a></div>
<div class="yv-pstep"><div class="yv-pt"><span class="yv-n">2</span>What it would change</div><p>Fewer farmed animals means less suffering, lower emissions, cleaner water and room for wildlife to come back.</p></div>
<div class="yv-pstep"><div class="yv-pt"><span class="yv-n">3</span>How to push for it</div><p>Support better standards for farmed animals, public money for plant-based food and nature restoration, and the organisations already working on it.</p></div>
</div>
