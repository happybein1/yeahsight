<p class="yv-lead">The planet is about 1.3 °C warmer, and warming continues as long as emissions do. Energy and food are the two biggest levers, for each of us and for society.</p>

<h2 class="yv-half">🙋 What I can do</h2>

<div class="yv-path">
<div class="yv-pstep"><div class="yv-pt"><span class="yv-n">1</span>Understand</div><p>Read <strong>Climate Change</strong>, or listen to it.</p><a class="yv-btn" href="/forces/climate-change/">Read · 4 min</a></div>
<div class="yv-pstep"><div class="yv-pt"><span class="yv-n">2</span>Try today</div><p>Pick one: a plant-based day each week, a train instead of a short flight, or switching to a green energy supplier.</p></div>
<div class="yv-pstep"><div class="yv-pt"><span class="yv-n">3</span>Keep it going</div><p>Talk about it with people around you, and support groups pushing for clean energy, such as <a href="https://citizensclimatelobby.org/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Citizens' Climate Lobby</a> or <a href="https://caneurope.org/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Climate Action Network Europe</a>.</p></div>
</div>

<h2 class="yv-half">🤝 What we can do as a society</h2>

<div class="yv-path">
<div class="yv-pstep"><div class="yv-pt"><span class="yv-n">1</span>The idea</div><p><strong>Clean energy everywhere, and a food system with far fewer animals.</strong> Solar and wind are now among the cheapest electricity in most of the world. A fair carbon price, with the money returned to people, makes clean choices the cheap ones.</p><a class="yv-btn yv-btn--ghost" href="/forces/clean-safe-energy/">The Energy Transition</a></div>
<div class="yv-pstep"><div class="yv-pt"><span class="yv-n">2</span>What it would change</div><p>Low-carbon sources already make just over 40% of the world's electricity. Food causes roughly a quarter to a third of greenhouse gas emissions, so changing what we eat matters almost as much as changing energy.</p></div>
<div class="yv-pstep"><div class="yv-pt"><span class="yv-n">3</span>How to push for it</div><p>Vote with climate in mind, ask your representatives for a fair carbon price and support for clean energy, and back the organisations already working on it.</p></div>
</div>
