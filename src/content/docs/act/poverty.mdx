<p class="yv-lead">Hundreds of millions of people still live in extreme poverty, and millions of children die each year from causes that are cheap to prevent. This is where help is best proven.</p>

<h2 class="yv-half">🙋 What I can do</h2>

<div class="yv-path">
<div class="yv-pstep"><div class="yv-pt"><span class="yv-n">1</span>Understand</div><p>Read <strong>Global Health and Extreme Poverty</strong>, or listen to it.</p><a class="yv-btn" href="/how-society-works/global-health-and-poverty/">Read · 4 min</a></div>
<div class="yv-pstep"><div class="yv-pt"><span class="yv-n">2</span>Try today</div><p>Give once to a well-tested charity. <a href="https://www.givewell.org/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">GiveWell</a> lists charities whose results are checked in careful studies, such as the <a href="https://www.againstmalaria.com/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Against Malaria Foundation</a>.</p></div>
<div class="yv-pstep"><div class="yv-pt"><span class="yv-n">3</span>Keep it going</div><p>Set up a small monthly donation. A few thousand euros, given well, can save a life.</p></div>
</div>

<h2 class="yv-half">🤝 What we can do as a society</h2>

<div class="yv-path">
<div class="yv-pstep"><div class="yv-pt"><span class="yv-n">1</span>The idea</div><p><strong>Basic needs met for everyone.</strong> HappyBein proposes a universal allowance that covers food, housing, health care and energy, and fairer sharing of the planet's resources between countries.</p><a class="yv-btn yv-btn--ghost" href="/sharing-fairly/universal-allowance/">Universal Allowance</a></div>
<div class="yv-pstep"><div class="yv-pt"><span class="yv-n">2</span>What it would change</div><p>About 2.1 billion people still lack safe drinking water and about 670 million have no electricity. Meeting basic needs first is the cheapest way to improve the most lives.</p></div>
<div class="yv-pstep"><div class="yv-pt"><span class="yv-n">3</span>How to push for it</div><p>Ask your country to keep its aid promises and spend them on what works, and support fair trade and tax rules for poorer countries.</p></div>
</div>
