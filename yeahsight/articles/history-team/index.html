History & Team

The HappyBein Project was born out of a recognized need for better use of our spare time, lack of motivation, and realization of the low hope and slow progress of our society towards an optimal world.

Although there is a lot of inspirational content and supporting tools available, a clear explanation of a sustainable life-enhancing world is missing. Such insight could potentially lead to increased hope, motivation, efficiency, and eventually enhanced individual and global wellbeing.

HappyBein is thus aiming to find a clear explanation of a sustainable life-enhancing world and describe it in a way that helps the most people. It would be great to turn this activity into one that enables the making of a living, but the right approach is still being sought.

Therefore, in 2022 the founder initially created the HappyBein Stichting foundation (mainly for anonimity) before realizing that a sole proprietorship was better suited, from a tax point of view) for market discovery.

The core content&tools were initially been developed and expanded by the addition of the webshop in 2023.

The team is currently composed of its founder and individuals providing support (big thanks!). Additionally, content formatting (especially videos) is being outsourced on platforms like Fiverr or Upwork.

As we would really welcome partnerships or just exchange of ideas, feel free to reach out via the contact form!
