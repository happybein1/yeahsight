Project & Solutions

The HappyBein projects aims at setting the foundation which efficiently bring people and organization to productively reach a life and a world as optimal as possible.

The overall website and content is in Beta (validation) phase. It is shared for the sake of pre-sharing and gathering support.

UpBase, the starting point of this project provides easily understandable core reference points for people and organization to develop and jointly strive to a fair and comfortable life that maximizes their chance for enhanced wellbeing. This section covers the following topics:
  • Universal Life Goals: describes well-being and purpose, tips for enhanced planning, habits, and collaboration skills.
  • Physiological Needs: for a comfortable and healthy life
  • Earth Land Split : how the earth land is currently split offers idea how these resources could be ditributed in the future
  • Social Structure : insights on recommended social organization, based on logic and current experience until now.
  • Economical System : explains how the economical system is useful  in order to better understadn potential solutions
UpNext, then reviews potential solutions of some of the main challenges our world is facing today, such as how to fairly distribute resources amongst beings, how to reach sustainable energy mangement, how to ensure fair treatment of all beings. Here are the current sub-sections:
  • Fair Space Sharing Provides a proposal towards a fair organization of space on earth
  • Clean and Safe Energy Provides a review of the general energy production as well as recommendation for where to go
  • Plant-Based Consumption Provides the pros and cons to plant based consumption, a way to move towards.
  • Enhanced Automation Reason why automation is beneficial for the world
  • Vision & Roadmap General overview of the current proposal towards a sustainable world
UpTools, finally,  provides a set of additional tool and follow up links to continue on your quest for enhanced wellbeing.
  • Daily Activities Overview of potential daily activiites ( if you are looking for something to do today)
  • Long-term Goals Overview Overview of potential long term Goals
  • External Project Tracker : tool intended to list relevant project for individual and general development
  • WebStore: Purchase and use branded merchandise such as T-shirts and mugs that promote a positive feeling.
  • Wellness Measurement Form which can help you measure your level of general wellbeing

Finally, please check the Collaborate with us page if you are interested in supporting or joining this project!
