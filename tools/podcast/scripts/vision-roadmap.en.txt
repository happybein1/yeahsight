# Vision and Roadmap

This is YeahSight, by HappyBein. In this episode: the Vision and Roadmap. A proposed direction for society, in six steps.

A quick note before we start. This is a proposal, not a finished plan. Some of these ideas still need more work and more evidence. They are here to be discussed, and improved.

## Towards an optimal world

The YeahSight articles look at how society works, and at the forces shaping its future. This roadmap brings those findings together, into one direction. Each step comes with background articles on the YeahSight website, if you want to go deeper.

## Step one. Support people's goals.

The first step is to help every person understand and pursue their own basic goals, through education and support. When people know what they need, and how to get there, they become more productive, and they feel better. And that speeds up positive change for everyone.

## Step two. Eat more plants, and give space back to nature.

The second step is to move towards plant-based food, and to give land back to nature. Today, most farmland is used to feed animals. Freeing part of that land would let wildlife thrive again, and would let forests store more carbon.

## Step three. Move to renewable energy.

The third step is to switch to renewable energy. Mostly solar power, combined with geothermal energy, and with wood or biomass to store energy from one season to the next.

Two levers could speed this up. First, a tax on fossil fuels, with the money going into renewables. Second, linking the price of energy to solar output. Energy would be cheapest when the sun produces the most, so people would naturally use it at those moments, and storing energy between seasons would become worth the investment.

## Step four. Guarantee a basic-needs budget.

The fourth step is a standard budget for every person, and every organisation, dedicated to basic needs. Food, energy, housing, education, health, and insurance. It works a bit like a universal income, but it can only be spent on those needs, and only for a limited time. So it always does what it is meant to do.

## Step five. Phase out financial inheritance.

The fifth step is to phase out financial inheritance and large donations. Wealth passed on privately is a major source of inequality and insecurity. Instead, accumulated wealth would return to a common fund, and be shared fairly through the public system, for example as a fair starting capital for every young adult.

## Step six. Use only what we need.

The sixth and last step is to bring the resources each of us uses down to a level that still gives enough comfort, and enough room to be productive. More efficient infrastructure, and the automation of tasks, make this possible.

## Why it matters

Together, these steps could make the world safer, and fairer, for people and for all living beings. Some of them are already happening, but slowly, held back by inequality and insecurity.

They will take time. But the more we support them, the faster they will come.
