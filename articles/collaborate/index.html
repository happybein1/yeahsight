Collaborating with us

Would you like to know how you can actively take part in this project? Here are a few options which can be interesting for you. You can: As an individual:
  1. Read and watch the current content on happybein.com
  2. Tell us what you like or suggest improvements via this online form.
  3. Follow our our LinkedIn Page
  4. Subscribe to our Newsletter (contact us)
  5. Purchase and use a branded good from our webstore
  6. Become an active member of the project. The panel of potential activities is large and include the development of partnerships with other entities, the webstore, the content (online article, videos..), the narketing and social page management, a public utility foundation to gather donations, and new offering
As an organization:
  1. Provide us with content to share on our LinkedIn Page
  2. Share our content as you see fit (contact us)
  3. Become an official supporter and we will share your support on a dedicated page. (contact us)
